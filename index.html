HOW TO PLAY

GOAL — Guide Echo through 11 chapters: run, hide, fight, and keep up the remembrance of Allah.

KEYBOARD — ← → or A D run · ↑, W or SPACE jump · ↓ or S slide · E calm on/off · J strike · K block · F talk · ESC pause

TOUCH — ◀ ▶ run · JUMP · SLIDE under low signs · CALM on/off

INFLUENCE — Something inside Echo clouds his judgement. Voices, stumbles and stress make it rise. When it is high, Echo feels heavy and sees things that are not there. When it is full, he freezes.

HIDE — From Chapter 2, men search the streets. Stand in a dark doorway and press ↓ (or SLIDE) to hide while they pass.

FIGHT — Once Echo has the pipe: J or X (STRIKE) to hit, hold K or Z (BLOCK) to defend. Blocking from the front stops a punch.

TAKEDOWN — With the pipe, creep up behind a searcher who hasn't seen Echo. When STRIKE glows, press it to take him down silently.

CALM — Press CALM to start reciting. Echo keeps moving while he seeks refuge in Allah, and the Influence falls. Reciting switches itself off after 15 seconds, even if the Influence is already empty (the gold line under the meter shows the time left), so press it again when you need it. Voices and stumbles also break his focus.

ENREY — When you play as Enrey, tap STRIKE again and again for a four-hit combo, and press TALK (F) near people to ask for clues.

BIKE — Hold → to ride faster and JUMP over cars, crates and canals. When scooters pull alongside, press STRIKE and Enrey kicks them off.
